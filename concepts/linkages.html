Linkages: How Bars Stay Level While Moving Up

Think first!Why does one robot hand dump its load as it rises, while another hand stays almost level?

Why This Matters

A lift is not useful if it dumps the Bean Bag right before scoring. Linkages help Flop move a tray or hand upward while keeping the game object controlled. Once you see the bar pattern, you can spot why some lifts are smooth scorers and others twist, bind, or tip the whole robot.

🔗 Linkage

A linkage is connected bars. The bars guide where the moving part is allowed to go.

▱ 4-bar

A parallelogram 4-bar can keep the end piece almost level while it rises.

⚠️ Tradeoffs

More links can reach higher, but they can add friction, flex, torque needs, and tipping risk.

❓ Think About This!

If your tray dumps the Bean Bag at the top, is the problem the driver, the tray angle, or the linkage shape?
