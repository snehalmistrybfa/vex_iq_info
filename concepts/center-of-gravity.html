Why Does My Robot Tip Over? Center of Gravity Explained

Think first!Why does a robot feel brave when its arm is down, then suddenly wobble when the arm rises?

🟪
balance dot

🎯 Balance point

Center of gravity is the spot where the robot's weight feels balanced.

🔲 Support polygon

The wheels make a floor shape. If the balance dot stays inside, the robot stays up.

⚡ Tipping edge

If the balance dot moves outside the wheel shape, gravity can pull the robot over.

❓ Think About This!

Would a wide, low robot or a skinny, tall robot feel safer when it turns quickly?

⭐ Try It! Move the Bean Bag

Tap a button. Watch the bag and balance dot move toward or away from the tipping edge.

Low is safest. The balance dot is deep inside the wheels.

🤖 Connect to Flop

Flop must drive, turn, collect, and score while its arm changes balance. Smooth driving and a low, wide base help it stay brave.

🔍 Reflect

  • If you could add weight anywhere on Flop, where would you put it to prevent tipping? Why?
  • What happens to the balance point when Flop's arm is fully extended forward?
