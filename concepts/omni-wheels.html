Omni Wheels: The Wheels That Slide Sideways

Think first!How can a wheel roll forward but also slide sideways without fighting the robot?

⇆

🛞 Traction wheels

They grip strongly. Great for pushing, but they can fight sideways turning.

🧃 Omni wheels

Little rollers let the wheel slide sideways more easily.

🧭 Holonomic

A holonomic drive can move in more directions without turning first.

❓ Think About This!

If you spend extra motors on sideways driving, what motors are left for the arm and intake?
