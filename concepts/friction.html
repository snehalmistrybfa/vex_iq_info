The Invisible Thief Called Friction

Think first!Where did the motor's power go if the wheel barely spins but nothing looks broken?

Too tight = rubbing = stolen power!

👟 Good friction

Wheels need grip. Intakes need grip. Claws need grip. Some friction helps robots play.

🧱 Bad friction

Crooked shafts, squeezed collars, rubbing gears, and bent frames waste motor effort.

🌀 Free-spin test

Spin the shaft by hand. If it stops fast, look for rubbing before changing code.

❓ Think About This!

If fixing spacers makes the arm move better, did the motor become stronger, or did you stop wasting force?
