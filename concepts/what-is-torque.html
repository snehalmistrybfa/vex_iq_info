Fast little gear + big gear = slower output, stronger turning force.

🔬 What is it?

Torque means turning force. It is the twist that helps a shaft, wheel, gear, or arm move.

📏 Why arms matter

A Bean Bag far from the pivot is harder to lift. Long arm means more torque needed.

⚡ Gear trade

Gear reduction trades speed for torque. The robot arm moves slower, but it can push harder.

❓ Think About This!

If a lift works with no Bean Bag but stops with one Bean Bag, should you first blame the code, the battery, or the gearing?
