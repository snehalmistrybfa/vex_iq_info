Why This Matters

Torque is what lets Flop lift a Bean Bag instead of just buzzing sadly at the bottom. When you choose gears, arm length, and motor speed, you are choosing whether your robot wins a pushing/lifting fight or stalls in front of the goal. Learn this and you can make Flop feel stronger without pretending the motor got bigger.

🔬 What is it?

Torque means turning force. It is the twist that helps a shaft, wheel, gear, or arm move.

📏 Why arms matter

A Bean Bag far from the pivot is harder to lift. Long arm means more torque needed.

⚡ Gear trade

Gear reduction trades speed for torque. The robot arm moves slower, but it can push harder.

❓ Think About This!

If a lift works with no Bean Bag but stops with one Bean Bag, should you first blame the code, the battery, or the gearing?
