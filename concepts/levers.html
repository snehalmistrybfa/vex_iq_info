Levers: How a Small Force Lifts a Big Load

Think first!Where should you push on a beam if you want one finger to lift something heavy?

effort ↓
load

⚖️ Class 1

Fulcrum in the middle, like a seesaw. Push one side, lift the other.

🛒 Class 2

Load in the middle, like a wheelbarrow. Great for lifting heavy stuff.

🎣 Class 3

Effort in the middle, like a fishing rod. It moves far and fast.

❓ Think About This!

If the fulcrum moves closer to the Bean Bag, does lifting get easier or does the bag move farther?

⭐ Try It! Move the Fulcrum

Slide the pivot. Close to the load makes lifting easier. Farther away can move the load farther.

🤖 Connect to Flop

When Flop lifts a Bean Bag, the arm is a turning beam. The farther the bag is from the pivot, the more torque the motor needs.
