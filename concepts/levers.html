Why This Matters

Every time Flop lifts a Bean Bag, the arm is playing a lever game. Put the pivot, motor, or load in the wrong place and the robot struggles; put them in the right place and a small motor can do a big job. This is how builders make a lift that scores instead of just looking tall.

⚖️ Class 1

Fulcrum in the middle, like a seesaw. Push one side, lift the other.

🛒 Class 2

Load in the middle, like a wheelbarrow. Great for lifting heavy stuff.

🎣 Class 3

Effort in the middle, like a fishing rod. It moves far and fast.

❓ Think About This!

If the fulcrum moves closer to the Bean Bag, does lifting get easier or does the bag move farther?
