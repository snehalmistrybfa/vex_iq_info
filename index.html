These pieces form the frame, chassis, bracing, and mechanism supports.

Structure

Beams

1x, 2x, 2x4, 2x6, 2x8, 2x12, and 4x4 beams for robot frames and mechanisms.

Structure

Plates

Flat pieces for reinforcement, electronics mounting, shields, and platforms.

Connectors

Pins & Capped Pins

Standard pins connect most parts; capped pins stop at the outer face.

Connectors

Corner Connectors

Angle connectors make 90, 30, 45, and 60 degree structures easier to build.

Tools

Pin Removal Tool

Use it to pull pins, pry pieces apart, or push pins out from the opposite side.

Structure

Standoffs & Connectors

Create space between beams and support perpendicular or layered structures.

Structure

Low-Profile Lock Beams

Compact lock beams help capture shafts and build swing arms in tight spaces.

Build tips from the Placer Robotics Academy

  • Build one short section at a time and ask a teammate to check it against the picture before moving on.
  • Join parts correctly by choosing the right pin type and checking orientation instead of forcing parts.
  • Make it strong and square with beams, plates, corners, and two-point connections.
  • Check motion before powering on by spinning gears and wheels by hand first.
  • Route cables carefully so wires stay away from gears, wheels, and arms.
  • Compare ideas with quick trials using the same test for each mechanism.
